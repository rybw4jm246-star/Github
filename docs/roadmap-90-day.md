## Format rotation (mix in throughout, not just Month 3)

Don't run every day as a straight explainer — rotate formats to maximize reach:

- **Explainers** — "What does this mantra actually mean?"
- **Myth vs Fact** — correct a specific common misconception.
- **Daily Wisdom** — one actionable lesson from a single mantra.
- **History Shorts** — 30–60 second clips introducing one concept.

Note: Day 01 (Nasadiya Sukta / Big Bang) and Day 41 (full Nasadiya Sukta deep
dive) are intentionally different episodes — Day 01 is the short myth-vs-fact
hook, Day 41 is the full creation-hymn explainer once the audience has more
context.
