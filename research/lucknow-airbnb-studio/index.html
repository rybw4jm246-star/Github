1. Executive verdict (one page)

LUCKNOW STUDIO AIRBNB: INVESTMENT VERDICT

Best micro-pocket: Vibhuti Khand core, Gomti Nagar. Specifically the 1 km pocket around DLF MyPad / Vibhuti Khand Road 6, bounded by Summit Building, One Awadh Center, Indira Gandhi Pratishthan and Gomti Nagar railway station. Second-best (high-occupancy): Medanta–Lulu pocket on Amar Shaheed Path: Sushant Golf City Sector A/B and Ansal API, within 1.5 km of Medanta Hospital and Lulu Mall. Third-best (value/emerging): Indira Nagar metro belt: Munshi Pulia to Bhootnath metro stations (Sectors 11–21, within 600 m of the Faizabad Road metro line). Built for mid-stays and relocation stays. Wildcard: Gomti Nagar Extension Sectors 4–7 near Ekana and Phoenix Palassio, but not inside Omaxe Hazratganj, which is already saturated.

Recommended property: studio of 400–550 sq ft carpet in a gated tower with a lift, DG backup, guards and a pool/gym. Ideally the building is a serviced-studio or mixed-use project, where STR use is the norm, not a fight with the RWA. Recommended rent: ≤ ₹15,000/month furnished (Vibhuti Khand). Never more than ₹18,000. Recommended purchase (if ever): ≤ ₹42 lakh all-in for ≥ 450 sq ft carpet. At today's asks (₹50–60 lakh) the STR yield does not justify buying. Expected ADR: ₹2,400–2,800 (target ₹2,600) M Expected occupancy: 45–55% in year 1 with professional execution; 60%+ only if you reach the top decile M/L Expected gross revenue: ≈ ₹38,000–42,000/month (base) L Expected net (rent model, ₹15k rent): ≈ ₹4,000–8,000/month L Expected net (owned, no loan): ≈ ₹19,000/month NOI, which is ≈ 3.9% net yield, below a plain long-term let (~4.2%) L Estimated setup cost (rent model): ₹3.3–4.0 lakh, including deposit Recommended model: RENT (pilot), not BUY

Investment rating: ⭐ 4.5 / 10 · Confidence: Medium-Low

Biggest opportunity: most of Lucknow's 515 active listings perform poorly (median occupancy ≈ 20%). A properly run, couple-safe, work-ready studio with 30+ five-star reviews can sit in the top quartile (38%+) and capture medical and corporate mid-stays that hotels serve badly.

Biggest risks: 1. Supply shock. Listings grew 164% YoY and ADR fell 40% YoY [H, AirROI]. 2. Regulation. The UP Homestay policy assumes the owner resides on the premises. A non-resident entire-studio STR in a residential society sits in a legal and RWA grey zone.

Final recommendation: RENT (12-month pilot) — DO NOT BUY for STR yield in 2026–27. Revisit buying only after 12 months of your own booking data.

2.2 Hotel benchmark

  • Horwath HTL: Lucknow branded hotels ran in the mid-to-upper 70s % occupancy (2024). Lucknow "drops below 70%" in the 2025 review, as new supply arrived. All-India 2025: 64% occupancy, ADR ₹8,624 H (Horwath HTL 2025, 2024 PDF, TravTalk).
  • Conclusion: demand for rooms in Lucknow is healthy. The STR problem is product fragmentation and oversupply of mediocre studios, not lack of travellers.

2.4 How I estimate occupancy (and why you should distrust anyone who gives you an exact number)

  • Review-rate method L: in India, about 40–60% of Airbnb stays leave a review. Bookings ≈ reviews ÷ 0.5. Nights ≈ bookings × 3.2 (AirROI LOS).
  • Example: a studio with 82 reviews over about 24 months ≈ 164 stays ≈ 525 nights ≈ 262 nights/yr ≈ ~70% occupancy. That is consistent with "top 10% ≥ 60%".
  • Example: a studio with 8 reviews in about 12 months ≈ 16 stays ≈ 51 nights ≈ ~14%. That is consistent with the median.
  • Listing age is not visible in the extracts, so these are illustrations, not measurements. Use this method yourself on live pages: listing ID magnitude, plus "joined in" and first-review date, gives you the age.
  • Never read calendar unavailability as booked.

13. Competitor deep dive and the winning formula

What top-reviewed hosts do (from the comp set)

Winning Lucknow Studio Formula

"Medical-and-corporate-first, couple-safe, never-hot, never-offline." - Guarantee comfort and connectivity: DG plus inverter for AC, dual internet, stated in the title. - Make arrival friction-free: smart lock plus a pre-registered guest pass with building security, and an arrival video sent 24 h before. - Accept couples, but with ID verification and a clear house-rules policy. FSSAI Only if you serve food/breakfast. Avoid serving food (provide a kitchen instead). H
Fire safety The policy requires basic fire safety. Install an extinguisher and smoke detector. M

Bottom line: in a normal residential society, an STR studio is legally grey and practically fragile (one RWA resolution can end it). In a mixed-use serviced-studio building, the practical risk is low and the legal risk is moderate. Only operate after: (a) landlord's written STR permission, (b) society/maintenance-agency written no-objection, and (c) UP Tourism registration or a lawyer's written view that your structure is compliant.

19. DO NOT INVEST IF…

  • The society or RWA has a written ban, or a pending resolution, on short stays, OYO or Airbnb.
  • The landlord won't sign an STR clause, or the lease forbids sub-letting / commercial use.
  • The building has no 24×7 guard, CCTV or controlled gate, or the guards are hostile to visitors.
  • Maintenance is > ₹5,000/month on a studio, or the building is on a commercial electricity meter and rent is > ₹13k.
  • The unit is a 3rd floor or higher walk-up, or reached through a narrow lane where cabs can't stop.
  • Parking is not available for at least one car.
  • The DG doesn't run the AC, or the area has documented frequent outages.
  • Your target building already has ≥ 10 active studio listings priced under ₹2,500 (the Omaxe Hazratganj pattern).
  • Comparable listings in the building average < 4.5★ or show "dirty / AC / water" complaints you can't fix structurally.
  • The achievable ADR (from 5 comps within 500 m) is < ₹2,000.
  • You cannot obtain UP Tourism registration, or a lawyer's written view that your structure is compliant.
  • The surrounding area is mid-construction (metro or road works) for the next 12 months. Check the East-West metro alignment for old-city pockets.
  • You intend to run it remotely without a paid co-host. That adds ~20% cost and pushes the base case to break-even.
